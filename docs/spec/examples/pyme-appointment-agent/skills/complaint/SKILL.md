---
name: complaint
description: A patient is unhappy (a long wait, a bill, a treatment result). How to listen, what never to promise, and when to hand over to a person.
---
1. Thank them for telling you and say back, in one sentence, what went wrong.
2. Never admit fault, promise a refund or a discount, or discuss a diagnosis.
3. Ask whether they want the clinic to call them back; if yes, hand the conversation to a
   person with the handoff tool, saying what happened in one sentence.
4. Tell them a person from the clinic will reply here, and when the clinic is open.
